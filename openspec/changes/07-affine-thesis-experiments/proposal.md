# Proposal: Phase 7 — Single-MZM Complex-Phasor Affine Bias Control Thesis Experiments (C1–C6)

## Why
The reworked split-board prototype (`20261110-底板` carrying `STM32H503CBT6` @ 250 MHz with 32 KiB SRAM / 128 KiB Flash and `LMR51430 VSYS = 5.339 V` direct to `+5V`, paired with `20261110-MZM` using `47 kΩ` AC-TIA, `100 nF C0G` DC-blocking/injection capacitors, `R15=5 kΩ / R16=3.3 kΩ` DC monitor, `ADS131M02 CLOCK=0x0322` @ `64 kSPS`, `U7` bypassed, and `TS5A23159` switches removed with fixed jumpers `DITHER1 -> VA (C48.1)`, `DITHER2 -> VC (C56.1)`, `VB` DC-only) passed Phase 4 closed-loop bring-up on 2026-11-26 (`Vpi ≈ 5382 mV`, `null ≈ -3029 mV`, `drops=0, crc_errs=0`).

To produce the complete, reproducible experimental dataset and firmware validation required for the Master's thesis ("Single-Channel MZM Complex-Phasor Affine Bias Control", targeting `ch03.tex` `tab:hardware_new_validation` and `ch05.tex` `tab:affine_experiment_sequence`, `tab:affine_final_accuracy`, `tab:affine_final_stability`, `tab:affine_final_rf`, plus `03-supplemental-experiments-plan.md` C1–C6), we need:
1. Lightweight diagnostic and synchronous window-acquisition CLI commands (`hw info`, `adc stat`, `adc dump`, `meas`, `step`, `telemetry`) within the `STM32H503CBT6` RAM/Flash and `15.625 µs` `DRDY#` ISR budgets;
2. Firmware upgrade of single-MZM calibration and closed-loop control to the thesis Chapter 5 algorithm (principal-axis phase alignment, `O(1)`-RAM Fitzgibbon ellipse + winding + synchronous `CH1` DC regression, pure circular PI without `spring` or `obs_dc` self-subtraction, post-write `T_wait` block discard, `r < 0.15` integrator freeze protection, and circle-residual EMA recalibration trigger);
3. A fail-safe Python host automation suite implementing **17-point In-Situ DC Phase-Shift Interferometry (In-Situ DC PSI)** on onboard `CH1`, safe `50 Ω` `RIGOL DG922 Pro` LAN SCPI control (`192.168.99.115:5025`), synchronous head-to-head benchmarking of B1/B2/B3/B4/Affine (frozen, per-state recalibrated, and `S3+` online tracking), circular statistics, and thesis-ready tables/figures;
4. Human-gated physical execution and archival of experiments C1–C6 with transparent bench boundary disclosure.

## Explicit Bench Boundaries and Out-of-Scope Items
- **No fabrication or simulation backfill**: Any metric requiring disconnected external lab instruments is strictly labeled `"待外部仪器补测"` or `"待 VOA 补测"` in reports and handoff tables:
  1. C1 external-instrument metrics: (a) `-10 ~ +10 V` absolute DC linearity (requires calibrated DMM); (b) electrode pilot absolute amplitude, phase, and THD (requires oscilloscope/spectrum analyzer); (c) bias electrode `1/2 kHz` voltage noise density; (d) power-rail ripple (`mVpp`) and IC temperature rise (`°C`).
  2. C6 `-10 dB` weak-light condition: the bench `1550 nm` laser has fixed output power and no external VOA is installed.
- **Onboard optical phase ground truth**: The `90%` main optical branch has no external PD or coherent receiver; C3–C6 prohibit single-point `arccos` lookup against historical extrema and standardize on **17-point In-Situ DC PSI** via onboard `CH1`, explicitly reporting fit uncertainty and pre/post 3 s drift bounds.
- **Coordinate convention**: Thesis uses cosine coordinates (`MATP=0°`, `QTP+=90°`, `MITP=180°`); firmware uses dark-null zero (`MIN=0°`, `QUAD=90°`, `MAX=180°`). All scripts, CSVs, and reports convert explicitly via $\phi_{\mathrm{thesis}} = \mathrm{wrap}(\phi_{\mathrm{fw}} + \pi)$.
- **Isolation**: `openspec/changes/05-tdm-multichannel-and-ui/`, legacy single-board compatibility, and external `MasterThesisTest/` files are untouched.
